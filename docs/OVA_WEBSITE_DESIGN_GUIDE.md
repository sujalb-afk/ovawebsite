# OVA Website Design Guide

Guidance for how the OVA site should look and behave, aligned with NGO best practices and our four objectives.

---

## OVA's Four Objectives (Scope)

1. **Sustainability awareness and mitigation**
2. **AI awareness and training**
3. **Job Readiness Programs**
4. **Charity services to the underprivileged rural students**

---

## How an NGO Website Like OVA Should Look & Behave

### 1. Mission and clarity (above the fold)

- **Clear mission statement** in the hero or header. Visitors spend ~54 seconds on a page; the purpose of the site must be obvious.
- **One primary action** per screen (e.g. Donate, Join, Learn more) with a visible, contrasting CTA.
- **No clutter**: use space to direct attention to mission and CTAs.

### 2. Structure and navigation

- **Simple, predictable navigation**: Home, About Us, Services, Events (and any other core sections). Minimal items; avoid long dropdowns.
- **Audience-aware paths**: different entry points for donors, volunteers, beneficiaries, and partners if needed.
- **Consistent layout**: same header/footer and section patterns across the four main pages.

### 3. Trust and credibility

- **Impact first**: numbers (people reached, events, trainings), short stories, and outcomes for each objective.
- **Testimonials / beneficiary voices**: real quotes or stories linked to sustainability, AI training, job readiness, or rural students.
- **Transparency**: who you are, where you work (e.g. Kolhapur / rural focus), and how to contact or partner.

### 4. Content per objective

- **Sustainability**: clear messaging on awareness + mitigation (e.g. tree planting, waste management, climate education). Use nature/community imagery.
- **AI awareness and training**: explain what you teach (e.g. digital literacy, AI basics, ethics). Use modern but approachable visuals and avoid jargon in headlines.
- **Job Readiness**: who it’s for (e.g. youth, women, recovering individuals), what skills/programs you offer, and how to join or refer someone.
- **Charity / rural students**: who is eligible, what support you give (e.g. education, materials, mentorship), and how to apply or donate for this cause.

### 5. Engagement and actions

- **Donate**: prominent in header/footer and near impact content; short, clear flow (few clicks).
- **Volunteer / Join**: visible path for people who want to contribute time or skills.
- **Newsletter or updates**: optional signup for news and events.

### 6. Visual and technical quality

- **Imagery**: real or representative photos of volunteers, communities, nature, and learning. Avoid generic stock-only look.
- **Mobile-first**: layout and CTAs must work on small screens; test on phones.
- **Performance**: optimize images and assets so the site loads quickly.
- **Accessibility**: readable contrast, clear labels, and keyboard/screen-reader friendly where possible.

### 7. Brand consistency

- **Logo and name** (e.g. Volunteer India | OVA) in header on every page.
- **Consistent colours and type**: one primary palette (e.g. green for sustainability/trust) and one main font family across the four pages.

---

## Applying This to the Four Pages

| Page       | Emphasize                                                                 |
|-----------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Home**  | One mission line; four objectives in short; hero CTA; impact numbers; testimonials; clear Donate/Join. |
| **About Us** | Who OVA is; why these four objectives; team/place (e.g. Kolhapur); story and values. |
| **Services** | Four sections: Sustainability, AI awareness & training, Job Readiness, Charity (rural students). Each with description, who it’s for, and a CTA. |
| **Events** | Upcoming and past events tied to the four objectives; clear dates and how to participate or support. |
